# Wild vampire bats — continuous proximity sensing

- Source archive: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.12045450.v5
- Dryad record: https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.g4f4qrfnx
- Associated study: https://doi.org/10.1093/beheco/araa111
- Study population: 31 adult female common vampire bats in one wild roost
- Temporal coverage: approximately three days after an immune challenge experiment
- Edge semantics: high-resolution sensor proximity, filtered by the authors' strict 85th-percentile RSSI threshold and fused with their published overlap rule
- License: CC BY 4.0 for the downloaded Figshare archive; the Dryad record is CC0
- Download status: complete on 2026-08-20

The LPS and saline assignments are retained as study context. They are not treated as infection states or empirical intervention outcomes. `raw/` is immutable source material. Derived files belong in `interim/` or `processed/`.
